Employees may file for sick leave for the following medical conditions.

Flu and common cold are covered under standard sick leave.

Migraine and chronic headache conditions are covered under standard sick leave.

Back injury and related musculoskeletal conditions are covered under standard sick leave.

Minor surgery recovery is covered under standard sick leave with a doctor's note.

Mental health leave is covered under standard sick leave and does not require disclosure of details.

To file for sick leave, follow these steps. Step 1: Notify your manager via Slack or email within the first 24 hours of absence. Step 2: Submit a sick leave request in the HR portal, selecting "Sick Leave" as the category. Step 3: If absence exceeds 3 consecutive days, attach a doctor's note to the request. Step 4: HR will confirm approval within 2 business days.
