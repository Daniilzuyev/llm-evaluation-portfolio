# Lost or stolen card

Step 1: Freeze the card immediately in the Imagine Bank mobile app under Cards > Freeze.

Step 2: Request a replacement in the app. A new card arrives within 5-7 business days.

Step 3: Review recent transactions and report any you do not recognize. Imagine Bank will never ask for your full card number or PIN.
