# Card fees

Imagine Bank Debit Card: the monthly maintenance fee is $2. The fee is waived if you receive at least $500 in deposits during the month.

Imagine Bank Credit Card: the monthly maintenance fee is $8. The fee is waived for customers who spend at least $1,000 on the card during the month.
